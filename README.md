## Moved to softops.tech

The company site now lives at **https://softops.tech** (repo `SoftOpsHub/softops.tech`).
`index.html` (also served as `404.html`) redirects every company page there, mapping old routes to
the matching section (`/contact` → `#contact`, `/work` → `#work`, service pages → `#capabilities`).

These stay served here because the published P2P Food app links to them:

| Path | Why |
|---|---|
| `/privacyPolicy`, `/termsAndConditions` | Legal pages opened from the app |
| `/delete-account.html`, `/delete-data.html` | Google Play account/data deletion URLs |
| `/p2pfood-admin/` | P2P Food admin panel |

Move these, update the app and its store listings, before retiring this repo or the domain.
